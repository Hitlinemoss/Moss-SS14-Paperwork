This document officially certifies that the designated individual is permitted by the stamping member(s) of Command to be a Visitor aboard their entrusted station. They are to be provided a Visitor ID card and passenger headset for the duration of their visit. As a Visitor, the designated individual is considered to be a member of the station's crew, and is therefore protected by (and subject to) Space Law.

[italic]The Head of Personnel, Captain, and/or other acting authority reserve the right to revoke this permit at their discretion. Unauthorized distribution of a station ID card or radio encryption key is grounds for disciplinary action, and may result in criminal charges if applicable.[/italic]
